🔴 POINT

A point is just one little dot.

POINT

A point is a single dot. It marks one exact spot — and we can give it a name, like Point A.

➡️ LINE

What do you think a line is made of?

LINE

Points join together — with no space between them — and they become one line.

➡️ RAY

What if the line begins at a point and goes only one way?

RAY

A ray has one end point. It starts there and goes on forever in one direction.

📐 ANGLE

Two rays that share the same end point make an angle. That shared point is the vertex.

📐 HOW BIG IS THE ANGLE?

Drag the yellow knob to tilt the ray. The angle grows from 0° all the way to 360°.

THREE KINDS OF ANGLES

Angles can be small, exactly a right angle, or wide open.

📐 ACUTE ANGLE

Smaller than a right angle — less than 90°.

You learned Point, Line, Ray & Angle! 🎉

Point = a dot · Line = 0 ends · Ray = 1 end · Angle = 2 rays joined at a vertex
